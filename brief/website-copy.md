Technology


Meet Maverick OS.
Manufacturing intelligence, developed for pharmaceutical production.

Maverick OS is Mustard Bio's proprietary manufacturing execution software, designed to
support the management and monitoring of pharmaceutical manufacturing workflows.

What Maverick OS can demonstrate today

The current software demonstration supports simulated pharmaceutical manufacturing
workflows, including batch creation, pre-start checks, electronic sign-offs, process monitoring,
electronic batch records and release-test checks.
These capabilities have been demonstrated in a simulated environment. Maverick OS is not yet
validated or deployed for routine production use.

What we're building next

Our development roadmap focuses on connecting Maverick OS to real manufacturing
environments, beginning with read-only machine data integration.

Subsequent development will address production-grade audit trails, electronic records, security,
reliability, quality workflows and the validation required for pharmaceutical operations.

Where we're going

Our longer-term roadmap moves from monitoring and operator decision support toward
supervised automation of approved manufacturing processes.

We ultimately intend to make Maverick OS available to other pharmaceutical manufacturers
through commercial software arrangements, subject to successful development and validation.
