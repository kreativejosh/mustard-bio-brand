# Mustard Bio — Comms and Marketing Brief

*(Text of `mustard-bio-comms-and-marketing-brief.pdf`.)*

## Vision
To become the intelligence layer powering the next generation of pharmaceutical factories worldwide.

We envision a future where pharmaceutical manufacturing is increasingly autonomous, intelligent, efficient, and accessible, transforming how essential medicines are produced and supplied globally.

## Mission
To develop, validate, and commercialise intelligent manufacturing systems that make pharmaceutical production faster, more efficient, reliable, and cost-effective.

We are building technology-enabled pharmaceutical factories, beginning in Nigeria, that serve two interconnected purposes:

1. **Commercial manufacturing:** Produce medicines for pharmaceutical companies and brands through contract and white-label manufacturing.
2. **Technology development:** Deploy, test, and continuously improve manufacturing software and automation systems in real-world production environments, with the goal of commercialising proprietary technology for other manufacturers.

## Long-Term Ambition
To establish Mustard Bio's technology as a foundational intelligence infrastructure for pharmaceutical manufacturing globally, with the potential to extend into other industries.

## Core Positioning
Mustard Bio is the intelligence layer for the pharmaceutical factories of the future.
